GOLGAPPA JUNCTION 1

Big flavours.
Easygoing vibes.

Golgappa Junction 1 is a casual street-food spot in Forest Hill, Melbourne. Its menu brings together chaat favourites like pani puri and samosa chaat, Indian snacks such as dabeli and vada pav, plus filling mains and Indo-Chinese comfort dishes.

The idea is simple: bold flavours, a relaxed stop, and food that’s easy to share with friends and family.
